`session.assert`, so a forbidden action fails even when the UI is bypassed.

## Record-level rules

<!-- protoyard:generated:rules -->
- `conversations:manage`
<!-- /protoyard:generated:rules -->

- `conversations:manage`: a `member` may rename, archive, unarchive or
  delete only conversations whose `createdBy` is their own persona id. The
  rule passes for every other role and when no conversation is given. The
  services `rename`, `archive` and `remove` pass the conversation to
  `session.assert`, and the conversation menu calls `can(…, conversation)`
  to hide what is not allowed.

Notes on what is not a rule today: pinning checks `messages:send` (any
member can pin any conversation they can open, and the flag is shared);
`cancel` and `markRead` have no capability check; agent replies are skipped
silently, not denied, when the persona lacks `agents:invoke`; and there is
no membership check on opening a conversation by id. Role labels in the UI
come from `ROLE_LABELS` (`viewer` is shown as "Guest (read-only)").

In the `active-team` family of scenarios the actor's role comes from the
scenario (`admin` for Alex Rivera in `active-team`, `owner` in
`empty-new-user`, `member` for Priya Shah in `member-view`, `viewer` for Kai
Tanaka in `viewer-view`). Switch personas in DevTools to see other roles.
