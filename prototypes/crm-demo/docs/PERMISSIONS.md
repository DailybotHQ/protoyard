`session.assert`, so a forbidden action fails even when the UI is bypassed.

## Record-level rules

<!-- protoyard:generated:rules -->
- `deals:delete`
- `deals:update`
<!-- /protoyard:generated:rules -->

A rule runs only after the role already holds the capability, and the
service passes the stored deal as the resource.

- `deals:update`: any role other than `rep` passes the rule (viewers are
  already blocked because they lack the capability). A `rep`
  passes only when the deal's `ownerId` equals their persona id. Used by
  `deals.update` and `deals.moveStage`, and by the screens to decide whether
  a card is draggable and whether Edit and the stage select appear.
- `deals:delete`: the same ownership check for `deals.remove`.

The rule also passes when no resource is given, so `can('deals:update')`
without a deal answers "can this role update deals at all". Companies,
contacts and activities have no record-level rule: a rep can edit any
company or contact and log activity on any deal.

Capabilities defined in `src/domain/policy.ts` that no role names
explicitly, so only `owner` and `admin` hold them through `*`:
`companies:delete`, `contacts:delete`, `team:manage`, `settings:manage`.
`team:manage` and `settings:manage` only change a notice on Team &
settings; no service uses them. The route capabilities `deals:read` and
`contacts:read` in [SCREENS.md](SCREENS.md) are metadata and are not
enforced.
