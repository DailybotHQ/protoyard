The SaaS kit's sign-in, sign-up, password reset, magic link, phone sign-in and code, MFA and profile forms, and the onboarding wizard, render through Texuli's `SchemaForm`. The wizard is one multi-step form whose steps save through the onboarding service. Simulated password fields use Texuli's password type with `autocomplete="off"`.
