# Screens

- **Overview**: project summaries and due-date metrics.
- **Project board**: project tasks grouped by status, with keyboard/pointer moves and saved order.
- **Timeline**: virtualized schedule bars with zoom, progress and saved rescheduling.
- **Workload**: open tasks and estimated hours per assignable persona, with service-backed assignment.
- **My tasks**: tasks assigned to the active persona, grouped by due date.
- **Task detail**: description, comments and activity history.

The board and timeline are lazy routes backed by `@protoyard/views-ui` subpaths.
