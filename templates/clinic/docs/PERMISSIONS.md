## Record-level rules

<!-- protoyard:generated:rules -->
_No record-level rules._
<!-- /protoyard:generated:rules -->

There are no policy rules. One record-level check lives in the services
instead: only the persona linked to the appointment's provider
(`providers.personaId`) can save or sign that visit's note. Admins can read
notes but never write them.

What each role sees:

- **Admin** (Dana Whitfield): everything except writing notes; opens the audit log.
- **Front desk** (Sam Okoro, the default): registers and edits patients, books, checks in and cancels, reveals sensitive details. Visit notes and the audit log stay hidden.
- **Clinician** (Dr. Mei Tan, Dr. Kwame Osei, Lucía Rivera): reads all notes, writes and signs their own, reveals sensitive details. Cannot book or check in.
- **Viewer** (Priya Das, read-only reviewer): reads the schedule, patients (sensitive fields masked) and the audit log. No notes, no changes.

All of this is a UX simulation. It is not access control for real health data.
