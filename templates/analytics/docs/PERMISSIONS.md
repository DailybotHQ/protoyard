## Record-level rules

<!-- protoyard:generated:rules -->
_No record-level rules._
<!-- /protoyard:generated:rules -->

There are no policy rules. Segment ownership is enforced in `src/domain/services.ts`: a member (`segments:manage-own`) may rename or delete a segment only when `segment.ownerId` is their persona id. Owners and admins hold `segments:manage-all` through `*`.
