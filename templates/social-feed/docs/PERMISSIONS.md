`session.assert`, so a forbidden action fails even when the UI is bypassed.

## Record-level rules

<!-- protoyard:generated:rules -->
_No record-level rules._
<!-- /protoyard:generated:rules -->

There are no policy rules. In `src/domain/services.ts`, `posts.remove` lets an author delete their own post. Anyone else's needs `posts:moderate` (moderators, through `*`). Nobody can follow themselves.
